Mockup V6 · Design system

Command Deck

A briefing screen in a hero tower. Graphite and a fine dot grid, panels with cut corners, sections that end on a slash, tall split-weight titles, and one power colour that floods whatever is active. The power colour always comes from the data: a title's Infinity Stone phase, or its reality.

01

Principles

  • One power colourEach screen has one accent, set from the content. Everything active borrows it: rail item, tab, button, bar.
  • Cut, never roundCorners are chamfered and section edges are slashed. No rounded cards, no pills.
  • Heavy meets thinTitles pair a 900 weight with a 300 weight in tall condensed type, so even long Marvel titles stay on screen.
  • Posters carry the colourInactive posters are greyscale; the selected one comes back in full colour.
  • No glowDepth comes from contrast, slashes and the dot grid. No blurred glows or glass.
02

Colour

Text colours are checked against --void for at least 4.5:1 contrast.
