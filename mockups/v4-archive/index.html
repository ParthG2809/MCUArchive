From the archive

Blade came first

Ten years before Iron Man launched the MCU, Wesley Snipes' Blade (1998) proved a Marvel character could carry a hit film. It sits at the very start of the Legacy files.

Story order starts in the 1940s

Captain America: The First Avenger is set during the Second World War, so anyone watching in story order starts there, not with Iron Man.

Three Spider-Men, one film

Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021) brought the Spider-Men of Tobey Maguire, Andrew Garfield and Tom Holland together, joining three separate film series in one story.
